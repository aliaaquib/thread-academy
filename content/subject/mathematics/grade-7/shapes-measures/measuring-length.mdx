## Key ideas

### Measuring length

Longer things cover more distance, and we measure length with centimetres and metres using a ruler. Always line up the 0 mark with one end.

### Comparing measures

Words like longer, heavier and holds more only make sense when you compare two things directly — always name both sides of the comparison.

**Key term — Capacity:** How much a container can hold, measured in millilitres or litres.

<WorkedExample title="Worked example: Measuring Length">
  Which is longer: 1 metre or 90 centimetres?

  1 metre — it equals 100 cm, which is more than 90 cm.

  **Answer:** 1 metre — it equals 100 cm, which is more than 90 cm.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Reading the wrong end of the ruler** Always start measuring from the 0 mark, not from the end of the ruler.
  - **Calling every four-sided shape a square** A square needs four equal sides and four right angles; a rectangle has opposite sides equal instead.
</ImportantNote>
