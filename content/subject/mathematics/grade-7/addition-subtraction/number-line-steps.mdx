This lesson focuses on Number Line Steps: jump forwards and backwards along a number line to add and subtract.

<Definition term="Number Line Steps">
  Jump forwards and backwards along a number line to add and subtract.
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Counting on and back

Start from the first number and count on for addition, or count back for subtraction. This is much quicker than counting everything from 1 each time.

### Counting all

Put two groups together and count everything from the start to find the total. This works well with small numbers and real objects like beads or blocks.

**Key term — Difference:** The answer when you subtract. It also tells you how far apart two numbers are, so the difference between 9 and 4 is 5.

<WorkedExample title="Shopping with coins">
  Lila has 7 pennies. Her grandma gives her 6 more. Then she spends 4 pennies. How many does she have left?

  Add the gift: 7 + 6 = 13.
  Subtract the spending: 13 − 4 = 9.

  **Answer:** 9 pennies.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Forgetting to count on from the bigger number** Start at the larger number and count on the smaller jump to save steps and avoid slips.
  - **Subtracting the bigger number from the smaller** Always check which number you start from — 9 − 5 is not the same as 5 − 9.
</ImportantNote>
