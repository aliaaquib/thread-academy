## Key ideas

### Angle facts

Angles on a straight line add to 180 degrees, and angles around a point add to 360 degrees. In a triangle the three interior angles always sum to 180 degrees, so one missing angle can be found by subtracting the other two from 180.

### Area formulae

Rectangle: base times height. Triangle: half times base times height, using the perpendicular height, not the slant side. Trapezium: half times the sum of the parallel sides times the distance between them.

**Key term — Angle:** The amount of turn between two lines that meet, measured in degrees. A right angle is exactly 90 degrees.

<WorkedExample title="Finding a missing angle">
  A triangle has two angles of 52° and 73°. Find the third angle.

  The three angles of any triangle add to 180°.
  Subtract the two known angles from 180°: 180 − 52 − 73.
  180 − 52 = 128, and 128 − 73 = 55.

  **Answer:** The third angle is 55°. Check: 52 + 73 + 55 = 180 ✓.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Using the slant height of a triangle as its height** The height must be perpendicular to the base; measure straight down to the base line, not along the slanted edge.
  - **Confusing radius and diameter in circle formulae** A = πr² uses the radius; C = πd uses the diameter. If given the diameter, halve it first for area.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="A triangle has base 10 cm and perpendicular height 6 cm. Find its area." hint="Area = ½ × base × height.">
    Area = ½ × 10 × 6 = 30 cm².
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Two angles on a straight line are 112° and x. Find x." hint="Angles on a straight line sum to 180°.">
    x = 180 − 112 = 68°.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Find the perimeter and area of a rectangle 7 cm by 4 cm." hint="Perimeter adds all sides; area = length × width.">
    Perimeter = 2(7 + 4) = 22 cm; area = 7 × 4 = 28 cm².
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Find the circumference of a circle with diameter 14 cm (use π = 3.14)." hint="C = πd.">
    C = 3.14 × 14 = 43.96 cm.
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
