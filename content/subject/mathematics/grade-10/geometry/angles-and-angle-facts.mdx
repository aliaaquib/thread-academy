## Facts about angles

Angles follow fixed rules, so you can often calculate an angle without measuring it. Angles on a straight line add up to 180°, and angles around a point add up to 360°. When two straight lines cross, the opposite angles are equal. The angles inside any triangle add up to 180°.

<Definition term="vertically opposite angles">
  The equal angles on opposite sides of the point where two straight lines cross.
</Definition>

## Parallel lines

Parallel lines never meet. When another line crosses them, it creates pairs of related angles. Corresponding angles are equal and form an F shape. Alternate angles are equal and form a Z shape. Co-interior angles add up to 180° and form a C shape.

<Definition term="alternate angles">
  Equal angles on opposite sides of a line crossing two parallel lines.
</Definition>

<WorkedExample title="Find a missing angle">
  A triangle has angles of 52° and 71°. Find the third angle, then the angle next to it on a straight line.

  ```maths
  third angle = 180 − 52 − 71 = 57°     angles in a triangle add to 180°
  next angle = 180 − 57 = 123°          angles on a straight line add to 180°
  ```

  **Answer:** 57° and 123°.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Diagrams can mislead">
  Diagrams are often not drawn to scale. Work out each angle from the facts instead of judging by eye.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="Two angles on a straight line are 110° and x. Find x." answer="x = 180° − 110° = 70°, because angles on a straight line add to 180°." />
  <PracticeItem question="Three angles around a point are 120°, 95° and y. Find y." answer="y = 360° − 120° − 95° = 145°, because angles around a point add to 360°." />
  <PracticeItem question="An isosceles triangle has a top angle of 40°. Find each base angle." answer="The base angles are equal: (180° − 40°) ÷ 2 = 70°." />
  <PracticeItem question="Two angles on a straight line are 2x + 10 and 3x − 5. Find x." answer="2x + 10 + 3x − 5 = 180, so 5x + 5 = 180, so 5x = 175 and x = 35." level="challenge" />
</PracticeQuestions>
