## Why we need an order

Look at 2 + 3 × 4. If you add first you get 20, but if you multiply first you get 14. Both cannot be right, so mathematicians agreed on one order. The correct answer is 14 because multiplication comes before addition.

<Definition term="BIDMAS">
  The order of operations: Brackets, Indices, Division and Multiplication, then Addition and Subtraction.
</Definition>

## Using BIDMAS

Division and multiplication have equal priority, so work them from left to right. Addition and subtraction also have equal priority, so work them from left to right as well. This means 24 ÷ 4 × 2 is 6 × 2, which equals 12.

<WorkedExample title="Brackets, a power and subtraction">
  Work out (4 + 3)² − 5.

  ```maths
  (4 + 3)² − 5
  7² − 5        brackets first
  49 − 5        then the power
  44            then the subtraction
  ```

  **Answer:** 44
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Equal priority">
  Multiplication does not always come before division. When they appear together, work from left to right.
</ImportantNote>

  <PracticeItem question="Work out 8 + 2 × 5." answer="2 × 5 = 10, then 8 + 10 = 18." />
  <PracticeItem question="Work out 3 × (6 − 2)²." answer="6 − 2 = 4, then 4² = 16, then 3 × 16 = 48." />
  <PracticeItem question="Work out 24 ÷ 4 × 2." answer="Work left to right: 24 ÷ 4 = 6, then 6 × 2 = 12." />
  <PracticeItem question="Work out 2 + 3 × (8 − 2)² ÷ 4." answer="8 − 2 = 6, then 6² = 36. 3 × 36 = 108, then 108 ÷ 4 = 27. Finally 2 + 27 = 29." level="challenge" />

<Summary>
  - Follow BIDMAS so everyone gets the same answer.
  - Work division and multiplication from left to right.
  - Work addition and subtraction from left to right.
  - Use brackets to change the order.
</Summary>
