This lesson focuses on Comparing Fractions: decide which is bigger: a half or a quarter?

<Definition term="Comparing Fractions">
  Decide which is bigger: a half or a quarter?
</Definition>

## Key ideas

### Quarter versus half

A quarter is smaller than a half, because splitting a whole into four parts makes each piece thinner than splitting it into two.

### Halves of numbers

Sharing equally between two people gives each person half, so half of 12 is 6. Finding halves is really division by 2.

**Key term — Half:** One of two equal parts of a whole. Two halves make the whole again.

<WorkedExample title="Sharing the cake">
  Four friends share a cake equally. The cake weighs 800 g. How much does each friend get?

  Four equal parts means each friend gets one quarter.
  Divide the whole: 800 ÷ 4 = 200.
  Check: 4 × 200 = 800 ✓.

  **Answer:** 200 g each — one quarter of the cake.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Common mistakes">
  - **Calling unequal pieces halves** The pieces must be exactly the same size for halves, quarters or any fraction to be correct.
  - **Thinking 1/4 is bigger than 1/2** The larger denominator means smaller pieces — fourths are thinner than halves.
</ImportantNote>

  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="A rectangle is split into 4 equal parts. How many parts make one quarter?" hint="A quarter means one of four equal parts.">
    1 — a quarter is 1 out of 4 equal parts.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Which is greater: 1/2 or 1/4?" hint="Draw both on identical circles.">
    1/2 — two parts make bigger pieces than four.
  </PracticeItem>
  <PracticeItem question="Half of a class of 24 children are girls. How many girls?" hint="Half means divide by 2.">
    12 — 24 ÷ 2 = 12.
  </PracticeItem>
</PracticeQuestions>
