## The probability scale

Probability measures how likely something is. It always lies between 0 and 1. A probability of 0 means the event is impossible, and 1 means it is certain. An event with probability 0.5 is as likely to happen as not to happen.

<Definition term="outcome">
  One possible result of an experiment, such as rolling a 4 on a dice.
</Definition>

<Definition term="event">
  One or more outcomes that you are interested in, such as rolling an even number.
</Definition>

## Calculating probability

When all outcomes are equally likely, use this formula:

<Formula label="Probability of an event">
  P(event) = number of favourable outcomes ÷ total number of outcomes
</Formula>

An event either happens or does not happen, so P(not A) = 1 − P(A).

## Relative frequency

Some probabilities cannot be calculated from equally likely outcomes. A drawing pin may land point up or point down, but those outcomes are not equally likely. We can estimate the probability from an experiment: relative frequency equals the number of successes divided by the number of trials. More trials usually give a better estimate.

<WorkedExample title="Coloured counters">
  A bag has 3 red, 5 blue and 2 green counters. One is taken at random. Find P(blue) and P(not green).

  ```maths
  total counters = 3 + 5 + 2 = 10
  P(blue) = 5/10 = 1/2
  P(green) = 2/10 = 1/5
  P(not green) = 1 − 1/5 = 4/5
  ```

  **Answer:** P(blue) = 1/2 and P(not green) = 4/5.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Check your answer">
  A probability can never be negative or greater than 1. If yours is, there is a mistake in the working.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="A fair dice is rolled. Find P(a number greater than 4)." answer="5 and 6 are favourable, so P = 2/6 = 1/3." />
  <PracticeItem question="The probability of rain is 0.35. Find the probability of no rain." answer="1 − 0.35 = 0.65." />
  <PracticeItem question="A pin lands point up 36 times in 120 throws. Find the relative frequency." answer="36/120 = 0.3." />
  <PracticeItem question="A spinner has P(red) = 2x, P(blue) = 3x and P(green) = 0.5. Find x." answer="2x + 3x + 0.5 = 1, so 5x = 0.5 and x = 0.1." level="challenge" />
</PracticeQuestions>

<Summary>
  - Probability runs from 0 to 1.
  - For equally likely outcomes, divide favourable outcomes by total outcomes.
  - P(not A) = 1 − P(A).
  - Relative frequency estimates probability from experiments.
</Summary>
