## Describing motion

Kinematics describes how objects move without asking what causes the motion. In a straight line we choose one direction as positive. Displacement and velocity then carry a sign, so a negative velocity means the object is moving in the opposite direction.

<Definition term="displacement">
  The distance from a starting point in a given direction.
</Definition>

<Definition term="acceleration">
  The rate of change of velocity, measured in metres per second squared.
</Definition>

## The suvat equations

When acceleration is constant, five quantities are linked: displacement s, initial velocity u, final velocity v, acceleration a and time t.

```maths
v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
s = ½(u + v)t
v² = u² + 2as
```

List the values you know, identify the one you need, and choose the equation that leaves out the unwanted quantity.

<WorkedExample title="An accelerating car">
  A car travelling at 5 m/s accelerates at 2 m/s² for 6 s. Find its final velocity and the distance travelled.

  ```maths
  u = 5, a = 2, t = 6
  v = u + at = 5 + 2 × 6 = 17 m/s
  s = ut + ½at² = 5 × 6 + ½ × 2 × 6² = 66 m
  ```

  **Answer:** 17 m/s and 66 m.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Check the conditions">
  The suvat equations work only when acceleration is constant. If an object moves under gravity, take a = 9.8 m/s² downwards and keep the signs consistent.
</ImportantNote>

## Practice

<PracticeQuestions>
  <PracticeItem question="A train slows from 30 m/s to 10 m/s in 40 s. Find its acceleration." answer="a = (v − u) ÷ t = (10 − 30) ÷ 40 = −0.5 m/s²." />
  <PracticeItem question="A cyclist accelerates from rest at 1.5 m/s² for 8 s. Find the distance travelled." answer="s = ut + ½at² = 0 + ½ × 1.5 × 8² = 48 m." />
  <PracticeItem question="A car moving at 20 m/s stops with deceleration 4 m/s². Find the stopping distance." answer="v² = u² + 2as gives 0 = 400 − 8s, so s = 50 m." />
  <PracticeItem question="A ball is thrown upwards at 14 m/s. Find the time to reach its highest point and the maximum height." answer="At the top v = 0. Time: 0 = 14 − 9.8t, so t ≈ 1.43 s. Height: 0 = 196 − 19.6s, so s = 10 m." level="challenge" />
</PracticeQuestions>
