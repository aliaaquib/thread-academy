## The pH scale

The pH scale shows how acidic or alkaline a solution is. It usually runs from 0 to 14. A solution with pH 7 is neutral. Solutions with a pH below 7 are acidic, and solutions with a pH above 7 are alkaline. Lemon juice is acidic, pure water is neutral, and bleach is strongly alkaline.

<Definition term="pH">
  A number that shows how acidic or alkaline a solution is.
</Definition>

<Definition term="indicator">
  A substance that changes colour depending on the pH of a solution.
</Definition>

## Measuring pH

Universal indicator shows many colours. Strong acids turn it red, weak acids turn it orange or yellow, neutral solutions turn it green, and alkalis turn it blue or purple. A pH meter gives a more exact number than matching a colour by eye.

<WorkedExample title="Identify a solution">
  Universal indicator turns purple in a cleaning solution. What can you say about it?

  Purple is at the alkaline end of the colour chart, which matches about pH 13.

  **Answer:** It is a strong alkali.
</WorkedExample>

<ImportantNote title="Lower does not mean weaker">
  Lower pH means more strongly acidic. pH 1 is much more acidic than pH 5.
</ImportantNote>

<ExamFocus
  items={[
